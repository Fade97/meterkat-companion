# Meterkat companion 1.4.1

Existing registrations move to the hosted relay's new address `https://relay.meterkat.fdu.gg` (staging `https://relay-staging.meterkat.fdu.gg`) on the next start. It is the same relay: the source, its credentials and paired phones stay as they are, and no re-pairing is needed.

New pairing links carry the new address. Meterkat for Android 1.1.1 and older refuses them until **Advanced: custom relay** allows `https://relay.meterkat.fdu.gg`; the next app update accepts them directly.
