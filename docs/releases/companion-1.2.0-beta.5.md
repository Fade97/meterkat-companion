**Beta.** Companions on 1.2.0-beta.4 or older don't update to it on their own; opt in with `meterkat update --prerelease` or download it below. **Update channel: Stable or Beta.** Under **Settings → Update channel** in the desktop app, or with `meterkat update channel stable|beta`, choose whether updates include betas. A beta installation stays on Beta, so from this version on it picks up the next beta by itself. Back on Stable, Meterkat keeps the installed beta until a newer release. Everything from [1.2.0-beta.4](companion-1.2.0-beta.4.md) is included.
