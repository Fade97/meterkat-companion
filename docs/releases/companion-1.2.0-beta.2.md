**Beta.** Installed companions don't update to it on their own; opt in with `meterkat update --prerelease` or download it below. **Desktop island:** click the drop (or an open notice) to see every provider at once: each window or quota bucket with its bar, percentage and time to reset. The panel closes on a second click or when the pointer leaves it. The island still never takes focus, and on Linux it now reads the pointer straight from X11. **Desktop: used or remaining.** *Settings → Coloured bars show* switches the window, the island and the tray menu between the used share and what is left, like the phone app. Everything from [1.2.0-beta.1](companion-1.2.0-beta.1.md) is included.
